Why awkward?

Because handmade should look handmade.

Small variations, unexpected textures, tiny creatures, fingerprints, crooked smiles — those aren't flaws. They're the parts that give each piece its character.

The goal isn't factory-perfect pottery. It's something you'll pick up ten years from now and still know exactly who made it.
